Reel Creator - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Reel Creator is a personal tool used only by its owner, Marcos Bellucci, to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account.

Data collected: the app only stores the TikTok access and refresh tokens issued to the owner after OAuth authorization. They are kept locally on the owner's computer.

Data sharing: the app does not collect data from any other person and does not sell, share or transmit data to third parties, other than sending the owner's videos to TikTok.

Deletion: the owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok settings. Contact: delbetu@gmail.com
